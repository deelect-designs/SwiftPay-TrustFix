# SwiftPay TrustFix — User Persona

## Ada Nwosu

**Age:** 29

**Occupation:** Digital entrepreneur

**Location:** Lagos, Nigeria

**Digital behaviour:** Regular user of mobile banking and fintech applications

---

## Background

Ada manages personal and business finances digitally.

She values speed, but financial security is more important when she is transferring money.

She expects financial applications to provide clear information before she authorizes a transaction.

---

# Goals

Ada wants to:

- send money quickly
- know exactly how much she will be charged
- verify the recipient
- receive immediate confirmation
- keep control of her financial information
- understand whether her transaction is secure

---

# Frustrations

Ada becomes uncomfortable when:

- fees appear unexpectedly
- transaction confirmations are vague
- recipient information is unclear
- security information is difficult to find
- error messages do not explain what happened
- she is unsure whether a transaction succeeded

---

# Behaviour

Ada tends to:

- check recipient information before sending money
- review transaction amounts
- look for confirmation messages
- compare financial information carefully
- hesitate when an interface feels ambiguous

---

# Core Need

> "Before I press Send, I want to be completely sure where my money is going and how much I will be charged."

---

# Design Opportunity

SwiftPay can build confidence by presenting the right information at the right moment.

The interface should help Ada answer five questions:

1. Is my account secure?
2. Who am I sending money to?
3. How much am I sending?
4. How much will I actually be charged?
5. Did my transaction succeed?

---

# Primary UX Need

**Confidence before commitment.**

The interface should allow Ada to make a financial decision without unnecessary uncertainty.
