SWIFTPAY TRUSTFIX

Data Privacy Audit

A privacy-by-design assessment for a fintech UX prototype, focused on transparency, data minimisation, security and user control.

Privacy Objective

SwiftPay should give users meaningful visibility and control over their personal and financial information while minimising unnecessary data collection.

Prototype Notice: This audit demonstrates privacy-conscious design principles. It does not claim legal compliance, certification or production security.

Frameworks Considered

NDPA

Nigeria personal-data protection and accountability.

GDPR

European personal-data protection and user rights.

CCPA / CPRA

California consumer privacy rights and controls.

ISO 27001

Information security management and risk controls.

ISO 27701

Privacy Information Management System.

Privacy-by-Design Controls

Data Minimisation

Collect and display only information required for a legitimate purpose.

Transparency

Explain security, transaction details and privacy practices at relevant moments.

User Control

Provide clear access to privacy and security settings and applicable privacy rights.

Security

Communicate important security states and design for appropriate production security controls.

Purpose Limitation

Personal information should have a defined, legitimate purpose.

Retention

Data should not be retained indefinitely without an appropriate documented purpose.
