- That the prototype reduced the 60% drop-off
- That users preferred the redesign
- That the design is legally compliant
- That the exact cause of the 60% drop-off is proven
- That real financial transactions occur
- That real user research was conducted unless it actually was

Use:

"reported 60% drop-off"

and:

"trust/uncertainty is a UX hypothesis requiring validation."
